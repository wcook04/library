Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations

  • Levent Alpöge
  • Tristan Buckmaster

Paper and Lean by Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster, from their repository tristanbuckmaster/navier-stokes at ad53419. Links, titles and notes made with AI. Full credit. Open PDF, 79 pages.

We prove that there exist smooth, compactly supported, divergence-free initial data on R3\mathbb R^3R3, and smooth divergence-free initial data on the torus T3\mathbb T^3T3, for which the solutions of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations without force blow up in finite time. This disproves the statements (A) and (B) of the Millennium Prize problem as formulated by Fefferman, and hence proves the statements (C) and (D). These solutions converge in self-similar variables to a backward discretely self-similar solution and blow up at its rate, which is (T−t)−1/4(T-t)^{-1/4}(T−t)−1/4 in H˙1\dot H^1H˙1. The discretely self-similar solution is obtained from a certificate: an approximate solution that satisfies explicit inequalities.
