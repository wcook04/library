The algebraic PCP theorem: a detailed account of the Lean proof

Explanatory reconstruction prepared by Will Cook with AI assistance · 16 November 2026.

Algebraic construction: Amireddy, Behera, Srinivasan, Sudan and Willumsgaard. Lean formalization: Troy Lee.

Abstract. The PCP theorem [AS98, ALMSS98] states that every language in NP has a polynomial-time verifier using O(log⁡n)O(\log n)O(logn) random bits and O(1)O(1)O(1) proof-bit queries, with perfect completeness and soundness error at most one half. We give a detailed exposition of Troy Lee’s Lean formalization of the algebraic construction of Amireddy, Behera, Srinivasan, Sudan and Willumsgaard. Set-multilinear encoding reduces the degree of line polynomials, and low-degree Hadamard encoding permits binary tests. We reconstruct the algebraic estimates and the machine-model arguments establishing the query, randomness and running-time bounds.
