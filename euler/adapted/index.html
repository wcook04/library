Finite-Time Singularity Formation for the Three-Dimensional Euler Equations

  • Levent Alpöge
  • Tristan Buckmaster
  • Matei P. Coiculescu

We construct smooth odd initial data on the three-dimensional torus whose solution of the unforced incompressible Euler equations develops a finite-time singularity. The vorticity magnitude at the stationary particle at the origin tends to infinity, while the velocity converges on the whole torus in every spatial CαC^\alphaCα norm with 0<α<5/60<\alpha<5/60<α<5/6. Away from the origin the velocity and its first time derivative converge locally in every spatial CmC^mCm norm. Each new wave has small velocity but a large gradient across its phase planes. The preceding flow amplifies the wave and turns its normal. After the amplitude peaks, the wave number is large enough to leave a strong shear in the orientation needed by the next wave. The launch times accumulate in finite time and the velocity increments are summable. To cancel the phase-averaged error of localization, we choose an initial velocity correction that fixes each particle’s final position to first order. A steep central slope in the wave profile supplies the shear. A shallow return slope bounds the positive pressure curvature in this endpoint problem.
