Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations

  • Levent Alpöge
  • Tristan Buckmaster

We prove that there exist smooth, compactly supported, divergence-free initial data on R3\mathbb R^3R3, and smooth divergence-free initial data on the torus T3\mathbb T^3T3, for which the solutions of the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations without force blow up in finite time. This disproves the statements (A) and (B) of the Millennium Prize problem as formulated by Fefferman, and hence proves the statements (C) and (D). These solutions converge in self-similar variables to a backward discretely self-similar solution and blow up at its rate, which is (T−t)−1/4(T-t)^{-1/4}(T−t)−1/4 in H˙1\dot H^1H˙1. The discretely self-similar solution is obtained from a certificate: an approximate solution that satisfies explicit inequalities. The certificate will be released soon; its hash is 3f7a335b84b6c7631742cacc5343dfcd11cf72f5221ff79b2b9268790b4b22c5.
