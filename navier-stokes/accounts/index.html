The strategy in six sentences. Work in X=H˙div1X=\dot H^1_{\mathrm{div}}X=H˙div1​, where the heat evolution after restoring similarity scale contracts by e−S/4e^{-S/4}e−S/4. The linearized evolution over a period is a compact perturbation of this contraction, leaving only finitely many directions that obstruct a chosen decay rate. A Lyapunov–Perron construction removes those directions by changing the datum in a finite-dimensional space of compactly supported fields, and a contraction solves the nonlinear perturbation problem. Cutting off a vector potential supplies finite-energy initial data, while a transferred right inverse and a second correction give an exact unforced solution on a large torus. Separately, a bordered linear problem, quantitative error bounds and a parameter fixed point turn an approximate profile into an exact one with zero auxiliary force. Regularization supplies the smooth decaying profile, and Appendix B reduces the required universal operator bounds to cell, mode, tail and matrix conditions on finitely represented data.
