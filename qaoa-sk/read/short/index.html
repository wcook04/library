Full support and positive density in the zero-temperature SK model

Will Cook · An account of the quantitative proof of OpenAI (2026a) · 19 November 2026

AI assistance. Will Cook directed the work. AI agents based on large language models produced and revised this manuscript.

Abstract. For the pure, zero-field Sherrington–Kirkpatrick model at zero temperature, the Stieltjes measure of the minimiser of the Parisi functional has full support on [0,1)[0,1)[0,1) and a smooth density that is strictly positive on (0,1)(0,1)(0,1). Full support and smoothness are due to H.-B. Chen. Strict positivity, which they do not imply, is due to OpenAI. We give an account of OpenAI’s proof, in which one coercive inequality between spatial moments of the Parisi solution is used twice: on a constant interval it excludes gaps, and at the minimiser it bounds the density from below. The inequality requires no minimality, which is what the second use needs. We compare it with the crossing argument of Chen’s proof, print the rational identity behind it in full, and work out the simplest finite-step case, in which the inequality holds with constant 2/52/52/5 and no better. The shape inequalities and the regularity theory are cited.
