Euler and Navier–Stokes reading editions

Will Cook prepared these independent v9 reading editions with Anthropic’s Claude Code and OpenAI’s Codex. The editions explain the authors’ arguments and provide links from numbered results to corresponding Lean declarations. See the 2:12 funnel video for how the editions were made.

Both sets of authors credit Anthropic for supporting the collaboration behind the original papers.

Finite-Time Singularity Formation for the Three-Dimensional Euler Equations

Original paper by Levent Alpöge, Tristan Buckmaster and Matei P. Coiculescu. The authors report that Anthropic models produced the first draft of the unforced proof and that they developed and revised the argument with Claude and Codex.

Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations

Original paper by Levent Alpöge and Tristan Buckmaster. The authors report using Anthropic’s Claude models throughout their work. Their Lean development derives the conclusions from certificate conditions, which the authors report checking by a separate interval-arithmetic computation.
