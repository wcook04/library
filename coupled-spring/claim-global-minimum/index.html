He formalised a general second-derivative test in a companion file, applied it to UUU, and describes the result as a proof of concept for the test.

For this function every quantity the test uses can be computed by hand, so the proof doubles as a worked example of the test. The sections below follow it in order: what the test requires (§1) and how its proof turns a positive Hessian into a minimum (§2). Then come a perturbed potential for which the completed square no longer settles the question (§3) and a hypothesis of the test’s remainder lemma that its proof does not need (§4). The last two sections derive UUU from three springs and describe the motion it governs.
